The .bin meshes in this folder are BodyParts3D models, converted to an indexed
float32/uint16 mesh for the browser:

  scapula-right.bin  FMA13395 right scapula
  humerus-right.bin  FMA23130 right humerus
  hand-right.bin     27 right-hand bones (carpals, metacarpals, phalanges)
  pelvis.bin         FMA16586 right hip bone + FMA16587 left hip bone + FMA16202 sacrum
  sacrum.bin         FMA16202 sacrum
